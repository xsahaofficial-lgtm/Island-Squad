# Island Squad

A naval tower defense game that runs inside a Reddit post. You build turrets around your island castle, hold off waves of enemy ships, and try to climb your community's leaderboard.

## The game

Open a post, tap Play and you're in. Before you start, the post shows today's top three players and your own best score.

Ships come at you from every side, and they go after your turrets as well as the castle. Spend gold on turrets, upgrade them between waves, and keep the castle standing as long as you can. When it finally falls you get one chance to continue.

It works on phones and computers. On a phone held upright the game turns sideways, so just rotate your phone. On screens that are close to square, the sea and the buttons stretch to fill the window while the play area stays about the same size for everyone.

## How to play

1. Tap Play on the title screen (or press Enter).
2. Pick a turret on the left bar, then tap the sea near your castle. If you tap a spot where nothing fits, the turret goes on the closest spot that does. Better turrets unlock in later waves.
3. Between waves, tap the green arrow above a turret to upgrade it. To turn a turret, select it, tap AIM (20 coins), rotate it, then press DONE.
4. The Target buttons on the right choose what your turrets shoot first: First (the oldest ship still afloat), Strong (the one with the most HP) or Close (the nearest).
5. Press Ready to call the next wave early.

On a computer: 1 to 6 build, Q and E rotate (after tapping AIM), Space pauses, Esc cancels.

You start every game with 240 coins.

### Power-ups

Shield works twice per game. Repair, Airstrike and Frigate work once each. They don't refill during a game, and the Frigate also costs 260 coins every time you send it out.

After waves 4, 10, 15 and 20 you may be offered 2X Gold, which doubles the coins you earn during the next wave. Your first one is free.

### The shop

Everything above is free and you can play the whole game without paying. If you want more, you can spend Reddit Gold. The game pauses while the shop is open.

- One more use of a power-up costs 25 Gold. In one game you can buy Shield once, Repair once, Airstrike twice and Frigate three times.
- 2X Gold costs 25 Gold after your free one, and you can buy it twice per game.
- 200 coins cost 25 Gold. You can buy this twice per game (400 coins).
- If a power-up needs more coins than you have, the shop shows how many you're short and sells just enough to cover it.
- When the castle falls you can continue once per game for 50 Gold. The castle comes back at half health with a short shield and the ships nearby get blown away.

## For moderators

Install the app and a game post is created for you. To add another one, open the community's mod tools menu and pick "Create an Island Squad post". Every post in your community shares the same leaderboards.

Two more items in that menu help you keep things clean:

- Island Squad: remove player from leaderboards (for cheaters or bad usernames)
- Island Squad: reset all leaderboards (asks you to confirm first)

Scores are checked on Reddit's servers before they're saved. Every game gets a one-time token, and a result is rejected if the time, kill count or score couldn't have happened in that game. No check is perfect, so use the remove tool if a score looks wrong to you.

## Your data

The game keeps only what a leaderboard needs, and only for the community you're playing in: your Reddit username, your best score, how many games you've played and when you last played. If you buy something it also keeps your extra power-up uses, any bought coins you haven't spent yet, and the order numbers. The payment itself is handled entirely by Reddit.

Players who aren't logged in can play, but their scores aren't saved. The game doesn't use ads, analytics or cookies, and it doesn't talk to any outside servers.
