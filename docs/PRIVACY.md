# Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Island Squad stores very little, and all of it stays inside Reddit's app storage for the community where the game is installed. The developer runs no servers of their own.

**What gets saved when you're logged in and finish a game**
- Your Reddit username and your best score. These show up publicly on that community's leaderboards.
- How many games you've played and when you last played (not shown to anyone).
- A one-time token for the game you're playing, which is deleted automatically within hours.
- Daily leaderboards are deleted after 14 days.

**If you buy something**
- Your extra power-up uses, any bought coins you haven't spent yet, and the Reddit order numbers (so a refund can take them back).
- Your payment details never reach the game. Reddit handles the payment.

**What isn't collected**
No email, location, device IDs, analytics, ad data or cookies. The game makes no requests to outside servers, and the fonts and images are bundled with it.

**Not logged in**
You can play, and nothing is saved.

**Removing your data**
Moderators can take a player off the leaderboards, or wipe them all. If you want your own data removed, contact the developer through Reddit.

Reddit's own privacy policy covers your use of Reddit itself: https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy
