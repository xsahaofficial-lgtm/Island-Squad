# Terms of use

Draft for you to review. Have it checked against Reddit's current rules and Earn terms before you turn payments on. This isn't legal advice.

Last updated: 2026-10-22

1. **The game.** Island Squad is free to play. You never need to buy anything to play it.
2. **Optional purchases.** You can spend Reddit Gold on extra power-up uses, 2X Gold, packs of in-game coins and a one-time Continue after your castle falls. Reddit handles the payment, and the developer never sees your payment details. Prices are shown before you pay. Coins you're missing for a purchase are sold in fixed Reddit price tiers, and any spare coins go to your game.
3. **What you get.** Bought items are consumable and belong to your Reddit account in the community where you bought them. Per game you can buy at most 1 extra Shield, 1 Repair, 2 Airstrikes, 3 Frigates, 2 more 2X Gold and 1 Continue, plus 2 coin packs. You can hold at most 3 unused extra uses of any one item. Items have no cash value and can't be transferred. Coins that were paid for but not yet added to a game are added at the start of your next one.
4. **Refunds.** Refunds follow Reddit's rules for Gold purchases. If Reddit refunds an order, what it delivered is taken back automatically. Anything you already used can't be returned.
5. **Fair play.** Don't cheat, tamper with the game or its requests, or abuse the leaderboards. Scores and players can be removed from the leaderboards by moderators or the developer.
6. **Changes.** Power-ups, prices and features can change. Items you already bought stay in your inventory unless they're refunded or have to be removed under the law or Reddit's rules.
7. **No warranty.** The game is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, the developer isn't liable for losses that come from using it.
8. **Contact.** Reach the developer through Reddit.
